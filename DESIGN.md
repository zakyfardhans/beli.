## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for effortless, trustworthy, and approachable universal commerce. The experience balances consumer delight-browsing, purchasing, and package tracking-with operational precision for merchants handling catalog items and order fulfillment.

### Design Movement

A synthesis of **Warm Minimalism** and **Tactile Modern Utility**. It avoids sterile corporate patterns through warm-tinted base grays, generous breathing room, and soft physical cues. Structural depth relies on crisp elevated white surfaces over muted warm slate grounds rather than heavy dividers.

### Emotional Attributes

- **Welcoming & Human:** Accessible, open, reassuring, and completely unpretentious.
- **Efficient & Reliable:** High legibility, crisp pricing structures, and scannable tracking states.
- **Calm Clarity:** Low visual fatigue during high-volume purchasing and administrative order handling.

## Colors

The palette establishes a serene foundation accented by a warm, approachable teal primary `#0D9488` and a focused utility blue `#2563EB`. Backgrounds avoid sterile cold whites in favor of soft stone/slate neutrals (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`), allowing elevated product cards (`#FFFFFF`) to surface naturally.

### Semantic Status Tiers

Order fulfillment relies on standardized semantic badge pairings (soft tinted background + deep saturated text):

- **Menunggu Pembayaran (Pending Payment):** Warm Amber background (`#FEF3C7`) with Amber text (`#B45309`).
- **Menunggu Verifikasi (Pending Verification):** Soft Violet/Indigo background (`#EDE9FE`) with Violet text (`#6D28D9`).
- **Diproses (Processing):** Airy Sky background (`#E0F2FE`) with Sky Blue text (`#0369A1`).
- **Dikirim (Shipped):** Deep Blue/Indigo background (`#E0E7FF`) with Indigo text (`#3730A3`).
- **Selesai (Completed):** Soft Mint/Emerald background (`#D1FAE5`) with Emerald text (`#047857`).
- **Dibatalkan (Cancelled):** Muted Rose background (`#FFE4E6`) with Crimson text (`#BE123C`).

### Contrast & Balance

High-contrast slate (`#1E293B`) anchors primary typography. Secondary labels use `#64748B`. Outlines and dividers stay subdued at `#E2E8F0`, maintaining a soft hierarchy without visual noise.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project an approachable, human, yet meticulously clean atmosphere.

### Hierarchy & Numerical Data

- **Price Displays:** Distinct weights (`700`) and tabular numeric support ensure quick visual scanning on catalog listing feeds and checkout order breakdowns.
- **Headlines:** Use tightened negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to avoid awkward breaks and establish strong visual weight.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Badges, buttons, and captions use medium-to-bold weights with slight positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) for legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout anchors the multi-device experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile surfaces:

- **Mobile (under 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Maximizes screen real estate for commerce cards and sticky primary bottom action bars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Dual-pane flows (cart details alongside checkout progression).
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column layout, max-width constrained to `1280px` centered, 48px canvas margins, 24px gutters.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px baseline grid (with a 4px half-step for compact metadata tags, icons, and badge alignments).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat, warm planes layered with ambient, soft shadows to create a light, floating feel.

### Surface Hierarchy

1. **Canvas Surface (Level 0):** Muted stone `#F8FAFC`. Base background for pages and side navigation backdrops.
2. **Elevated Card Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` card elements. Paired with a delicate, translucent slate border (`#E2E8F0` at 1px) and an extra-diffused low-contrast shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive Hover / Floating Popover (Level 2):** Elevated cards on hover, dropdown menus, and cart summaries: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.
4. **Modal / Slide-Over Drawer (Level 3):** Checkout sheets, product preview modals, and order drawer panels: `0 20px 35px -6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system emphasizes friendly roundedness that eliminates sharp corners while preserving structural clarity.

- **Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px maximum):** Used for product cards, table cards, admin summary blocks, promo banners, and checkout panels. Avoid larger radii to keep surfaces crisp.
- **Controls & Form Elements (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Form fields, quantity increment controls, and utility dropdown triggers.
- **Pill Shapes (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for status badges, tags, chip filters, and round icon action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Solid teal (`#0D9488`), white text, `8px` roundedness, medium weight. Subtle scale transition (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **Secondary:** Light stone tint (`#F1F5F9`) with slate label (`#1E293B`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent container, `#64748B` default text shifting to `#0D9488` on hover.
- **Mobile Fixed CTA:** Full-width sticky checkout/cart button elevated at the bottom viewport on mobile devices with an integrated safe-area buffer.

### Status Badges

Rendered as pill shapes with 4px vertical and 10px horizontal padding, utilizing the semantic color matrix:

- `Menunggu Pembayaran`: `#FEF3C7` background / `#B45309` text.
- `Menunggu Verifikasi`: `#EDE9FE` background / `#6D28D9` text.
- `Diproses`: `#E0F2FE` background / `#0369A1` text.
- `Dikirim`: `#E0E7FF` background / `#3730A3` text.
- `Selesai`: `#D1FAE5` background / `#047857` text.
- `Dibatalkan`: `#FFE4E6` background / `#BE123C` text.

### Product & Order Cards

White background (`#FFFFFF`), at most `16px` border-radius, thin 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `16px` padding. Media containers use a smaller radius with an off-white `#F8FAFC` image backdrop to cleanly isolate transparent product assets.

### Input Fields & Controls

- **Inputs:** Crisp white surface, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px radius, `14px` font size. Focused state introduces a smooth 2px `#0D9488` ring with a `15%` opacity expansion.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Teal accent fills with clean white checks, gentle 4px radius on checkboxes.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Integrated pill or soft rectangular container enclosing minus, count, and plus controls with a neutral background (`#F1F5F9`).

### Administrative Data Lists

Clean alternating hover rows (`#F8FAFC`), crisp tabular currency displays, status badges pinned to consistent right-aligned columns, and inline quick-action dropdowns.
